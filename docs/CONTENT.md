## Calling at Nigerian ports `/port-call-guide`
Meta: How a port call works in Nigeria | Lighthouse Maritime Agency. The eight stages of a Nigerian port call: what happens, which documents you need and what your agent reports at each step.

- Eyebrow: Guide · For owners, operators and charterers. H1: Calling at Nigerian ports, *step by step.*
- Lead: The eight stages of a port call in Nigeria: what happens, which documents are needed, and what Lighthouse reports to you at each point.
- Side card: Last reviewed · [Month 2026] / Port call checklist / Every document in this guide on one page. / [Download PDF]
- Stages (each block has: phase label, H2, body, "You send" or "Documents", "Who's involved" chips, "You receive"):
  1. **Nomination and PDA** (Phase 1 · Before arrival): You appoint Lighthouse as agent. We prepare a proforma disbursement account: an itemised estimate of port dues, pilotage, towage, agency fees and other charges, which you fund before arrival. You send: Vessel particulars, ETA, cargo details, purpose of call. Who's involved: Owner / operator, Lighthouse. You receive: the PDA.
  2. **Pre-arrival**: We file the Ship Entry Notice with the Nigerian Ports Authority and submit the cargo manifest and stowage plan to Customs to obtain the vessel's rotation number. You send: Cargo manifest, stowage plan, IMO number, last ports of call. Who's involved: NPA, Customs. You receive: arrival checklist.
  3. **Berthing**: We declare the vessel at the port's berthing meeting, secure the Port Manager's approval and book the vessel's movement. You send: Updated ETA and draft. Who's involved: NPA Port Manager, Terminal. You receive: berth confirmation.
  4. **Arrival** (Phase 2 · In port): With the provisional bill paid, pilotage and towage bring the vessel safely alongside. Tankers also require naval clearance. Documents: Provisional bill and receipt, master's declaration, pilotage chit. Who's involved: Pilots, Tugs, Navy (tankers). You receive: all-fast report.
  5. **Clearance**: Boarding agencies clear the vessel. We provide each with the master's lists and certificates and accompany Customs officers on board. Documents: Crew list, nil lists, port of call list, ISPS certificate. Who's involved: Port Health, Immigration, Customs, NDLEA, NAQS, NIMASA, DSS. You receive: clearance confirmed.
  6. **Operations**: Cargo is worked with the terminal and stevedores, while we handle crew changes, provisions, spares and any other husbandry needs. Documents: Discharge and loading lists, landing tally records. Who's involved: Terminal, Stevedores, Receivers. You receive: daily status report.
  7. **Sailing** (Phase 3 · Departure): We obtain NIMASA sailing clearance, settle outstanding port charges and book outward pilotage. Documents: NIMASA clearance, settled NPA bills. Who's involved: NIMASA, NPA, Pilots. You receive: sailing report.
  8. **Final account**: We issue the final disbursement account, reconciled line by line against the PDA, with receipts for every charge and any balance refunded or invoiced. You receive: the FDA.
