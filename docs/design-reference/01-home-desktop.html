03 — How a port call works

You always know where your vessel stands, from nomination to final account.

Read the port call guide

Phase 1

Stages 01–03

Before arrival

  1. 01

    Nomination and PDA

    You appoint us; we send a detailed port cost estimate.

    You receive: the PDA

  2. 02

    Pre-arrival

    Ship Entry Notice filed; Customs rotation number obtained.

    You receive: arrival checklist

  3. 03

    Berthing

    Vessel declared at the berthing meeting; berth approved.

    You receive: berth confirmation

Phase 2

Stages 04–06

In port

  1. 04

    Arrival

    Pilotage, towage and safe berthing alongside.

    You receive: all-fast report

  2. 05

    Clearance

    Port Health, Immigration, Customs and other agencies cleared.

    You receive: clearance confirmed

  3. 06

    Operations

    Cargo, crew changes and supplies handled alongside.

    You receive: daily status report

Phase 3

Stages 07–08

Departure

  1. 07

    Sailing

    NIMASA clearance and pilotage out of port.

    You receive: sailing report

  2. 08

    Final account

    FDA reconciled against the PDA, with receipts.

    You receive: the FDA

Cost and updates, in writing

Every report goes to you by email and WhatsApp, so nothing depends on a phone call.
