01 — Our story

Owners and operators calling at Nigerian ports often struggle with unclear costs and long silences. Lighthouse was founded to change that.

Incorporated in September 2024 and based at 5 Bornu Crescent, Apapa, we provide maritime agency, vessel coordination, procurement and logistics support across the country's ports. Like the light our name comes from, our job is to guide each vessel in safely and make every step visible to the people responsible for it.

Our vision

To be the leading maritime agency recognised for reliability and innovation.

Our mission

To deliver reliable maritime, logistics and procurement solutions with integrity and efficiency.
