Calling at Nigerian ports, step by step.

The eight stages of a port call in Nigeria: what happens, which documents are needed, and what Lighthouse reports to you at each point.

01

Phase 1 · Before arrival

Nomination and PDA

You appoint Lighthouse as agent. We prepare a proforma disbursement account: an itemised estimate of port dues, pilotage, towage, agency fees and other charges, which you fund before arrival.

You send

Vessel particulars, ETA, cargo details, purpose of call

Who's involved

Owner / operatorLighthouse

You receive: the PDA

02

Phase 1 · Before arrival

Pre-arrival

We file the Ship Entry Notice with the Nigerian Ports Authority and submit the cargo manifest and stowage plan to Customs to obtain the vessel's rotation number.

You send

Cargo manifest, stowage plan, IMO number, last ports of call

Who's involved

NPACustoms

You receive: arrival checklist

03

Phase 1 · Before arrival

Berthing

We declare the vessel at the port's berthing meeting, secure the Port Manager's approval and book the vessel's movement.

You send

Updated ETA and draft

Who's involved

NPA Port ManagerTerminal

You receive: berth confirmation

04

Phase 2 · In port

Arrival

With the provisional bill paid, pilotage and towage bring the vessel safely alongside. Tankers also require naval clearance.

Documents

Provisional bill and receipt, master's declaration, pilotage chit

Who's involved

PilotsTugsNavy (tankers)

You receive: all-fast report

05

Phase 2 · In port

Clearance

Boarding agencies clear the vessel. We provide each with the master's lists and certificates and accompany Customs officers on board.

Documents

Crew list, nil lists, port of call list, ISPS certificate

Who's involved

Port HealthImmigrationCustomsNDLEANAQSNIMASADSS

You receive: clearance confirmed

06

Phase 2 · In port

Operations

Cargo is worked with the terminal and stevedores, while we handle crew changes, provisions, spares and any other husbandry needs.

Documents

Discharge and loading lists, landing tally records

Who's involved

TerminalStevedoresReceivers

You receive: daily status report

07

Phase 3 · Departure

Sailing

We obtain NIMASA sailing clearance, settle outstanding port charges and book outward pilotage.

Documents

NIMASA clearance, settled NPA bills

Who's involved

NIMASANPAPilots

You receive: sailing report

08

Phase 3 · Departure

Final account

We issue the final disbursement account, reconciled line by line against the PDA, with receipts for every charge and any balance refunded or invoiced.

You receive: the FDA
