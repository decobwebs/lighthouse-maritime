Port agency, from nomination to final account.

We act as your vessel's representative in any Nigerian port: documentation, authorities, berthing and every cost, handled by one accountable officer.

What's included

Everything a port call needs, handled for you.

Appoint us as full agent, or as protecting agent alongside the owner's or charterer's nominated agent.

PDA and FDA

Itemised estimate before arrival; reconciled final account after sailing.

Pre-arrival filings

Ship Entry Notice, manifest submission and Customs rotation number.

Berthing and pilotage

Berthing meeting, pilot and tug booking, movement planning.

Boarding clearance

Port Health, Immigration, Customs, NDLEA, NAQS, NIMASA and DSS.

Cargo coordination

Liaison with terminal, stevedores and receivers; documents in order.

Sailing clearance

NIMASA clearance, port dues settled, outward pilotage.
